About Evergreen

Local lawn care built on showing up and doing the work right.

Evergreen Lawn & Landscape is a locally owned residential lawn care and landscaping company serving homeowners across Manassas, Gainesville, Haymarket, Bristow, Centreville and nearby areas.

Started in 2019

From side work to a local business.

Daniel Carter started Evergreen by mowing lawns and doing yard work on the side. As referrals brought in more regular customers, the work grew into a full lawn and landscape business.

That hands-on beginning still shapes how Evergreen operates. Daniel remains involved in the field with the crew, and customers can communicate directly with the business rather than feeling routed through a large company.
